08Handoff notes
SystemNewsreader display (hero 92/44, italics for emotional words) · Manrope UI (14 body, 13 meta, 12 floor) · Tiro Devanagari Hindi display · Mukta UI. Radius 20 cards, 14 buttons. Glass = white 6% + blur 20. Shadow 0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,.5). Pink + gold ≤ 12% of any screen. Reduced motion kills float, shimmer, glow drift.
ImageryEvery portrait frame is an image-slot: drop a commissioned original portrait and it persists. Brief for artists: adults 25+, clothed (saree, lehenga, kurta, slip dress, blazer), warm rim light, shallow DOF, eye contact or half-smile over the shoulder; sets per the brief. No real-person likeness.
Still disabled by designVoice call, video, checkout, create-companion are previews or waitlists. Helplines, grievance contact, entity, effective dates = data-driven TODOs. UPI marks are generic until a provider is approved. “Chats tonight” counts must come from real aggregate data; never fabricate presence.
